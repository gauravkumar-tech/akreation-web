A pre-wedding shoot is one of the most enjoyable parts of the wedding journey. It's a chance to get comfortable in front of the camera, try out locations, and create beautiful images that tell your love story.

## 1. Choose the Right Location

Delhi offers incredible diversity — from the Mughal grandeur of Lodhi Garden and Humayun's Tomb to the modern elegance of Connaught Place. Pick a location that reflects your story.

## 2. Time It Right

Golden hour — the hour after sunrise and before sunset — gives you the most flattering, warm light. In Delhi, that's typically 6:00–7:00 AM or 4:30–5:30 PM depending on the season.

## 3. Coordinate, Don't Match

Wear complementary colors rather than identical outfits. Think about how your clothes will look against the background.

## 4. Bring Multiple Outfits

Plan 2–3 outfit changes. Start casual, move to semi-formal, and end with something dramatic.

## 5. Be Yourselves

The best pre-wedding photos happen when you forget the camera is there. Laugh, talk, walk together — we'll capture the magic.
