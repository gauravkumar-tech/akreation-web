Every wedding is different, but the energy and excitement are always the same. Here's what a typical day looks like for our team.

## 5:00 AM — Getting Ready

The alarm goes off early. While the couple is still sleeping, we're checking gear, reviewing the shot list, and planning the day's timeline.

## 8:00 AM — Bridal Prep

We arrive at the bride's location to capture the getting-ready moments — the mehendi, the jewelry, the candid laughter with bridesmaids.

## 12:00 PM — Baraat

The groom's procession is pure energy. We position ourselves to capture both the grand entrance and the intimate family reactions.

## 2:00 PM — Ceremony

This is where we work as a team — one photographer focuses on the couple, the other captures guest reactions and details.

## 6:00 PM — Golden Hour Portraits

The best light of the day. We steal the couple away for 15–20 minutes of portraits that will become the centerpiece of their album.

## 9:00 PM — Reception

The celebration continues. First dance, cake cutting, dance floor energy — we capture it all until the last guest leaves.
