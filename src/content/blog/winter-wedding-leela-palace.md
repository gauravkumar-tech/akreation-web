Priya and Rahul's wedding at The Leela Palace was nothing short of magical. The crisp winter air, the golden afternoon light filtering through the palace windows, and the genuine joy on every face — it was a photographer's dream.

## The Ceremony

The ceremony took place in the palace's grand ballroom, decorated with thousands of marigolds and roses. As Priya walked down the aisle, Rahul's reaction was one of the most genuine moments we've ever captured.

## The Reception

The reception was a celebration of love, laughter, and incredible food. The couple's first dance under a canopy of fairy lights was the highlight of the evening.

## Advice for Couples Planning a Winter Wedding

- **Book early** — winter dates in Delhi fill up fast
- **Plan for golden hour** — the light in November is extraordinary
- **Have a backup indoor option** — Delhi fog can be unpredictable
